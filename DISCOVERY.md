## Specificity Test — Why 19?

The fractal criteria were applied, without any modification, **to every modulus from 3 to 40** (S1: family ≥ 2×expected · S2: m² | Σt · S3: 50/50 parity split + equal halves · S4: halves multiples of m² · S5: coefficients form a consecutive band · S6: Two-Hands union has exactly m members · S7: m | Σa(union) · S8: the two hands disjoint):

- *Caveat (October 20, 2026, note 21):* 8/8 is reachable only for the 12 odd moduli 17–39 (S1 with S6 requires m² ≥ 228, S3 an odd m); at an odd m, S4 follows from S2 and S3. The real number of rivals is 11, not 37, and the profile is seven independent conditions.

**Score distribution (200,000 synthetics, each granted its best modulus):** typical maximum score 2–3 (95%); 4: 2.4%; 5: 0.07%; 6: 0.005%; **7 and above: zero in this sample** (2.4×10⁻⁷ at 50 million — note 13).
